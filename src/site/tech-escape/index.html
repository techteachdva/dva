WHAT WILL I LEARN?

Every Chromebook in the lab quizzes you on real Middle School Technology content. Get it right and you earn a piece of the code. Get it wrong and the noise brings something running.

The Design Process

DEFINE the problem → PREPARE a plan and research → TRY a prototype → REFLECT on the results and iterate. Terminal 1 drills this cycle hard, including what belongs in each phase and what does not.

Code.org Middle School strands

  • Computing Systems — hardware, software, input/output, troubleshooting.
  • Networks & the Internet — packets, protocols, addressing, encryption.
  • Data & Analysis — collection, bias, visualization, models.
  • Algorithms & Programming — loops, conditionals, variables, debugging.
  • Impacts of Computing — digital citizenship, safety, ethics, AI.

ISTE Standards for Students (2025)

  • Empowered Learner — set goals, use feedback, choose the right tool.
  • Digital Citizen — manage your footprint, credit sources, stay safe.
  • Knowledge Constructor — evaluate sources, build meaning from evidence.
  • Innovative Designer — use a design process, tolerate ambiguity, iterate.
  • Computational Thinker — decompose, find patterns, test with data.
  • Creative Communicator — pick formats and remix responsibly.
  • Global Collaborator — work with others toward a shared goal.
