# Somnia

A cooperative psychic adventure — escape the collapsing Dreamscape before time runs out.

- [Play Somnia Online](/somnia/)

## About

**Somnia** is a tabletop cooperative roguelike for 1–6 players. You are Dreamers trapped in a dream. Acquire Archetypes by completing quests before the Dream Deck runs out.

## First session

New players should start in **Tutorial Mode** (two guided rounds), then a **Daydream**.

1. **Tutorial** — Visionary + Immovable, Quiet Dreams, The Innocent. Round 1 teaches Reveal / Explore / Meet and Accept. Round 2 teaches Landscape Action A, quest marks, and Acquire.
2. **First Daydream** — 12 points, 14 Dreams. The first game pins Innocent and a Quiet opening Dream. The Guide strip at the top of the map names the next step.
3. **Win** — Reach the point goal, then get every living Dreamer onto **The Bed**. **Lose** if the Dream Deck empties first.

Recommended first pair: **The Visionary** (Lucidity) and **The Immovable** (Willpower). Elasticity comes from the cards in hand.

Bosses, death, Objects, Trade, and Final Recurrence are in Overview → Details. They are not on the required tutorial path.
