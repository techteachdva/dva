Games Mr. Phil has made

FELECO

Bet on the species that will own the field. Seasons turn. A population that outgrows its food crashes, and no species is promised the win.

Campaign

Pledge to one species and survive ten eras. The danger steps up when you do.

Challenge

A set field and a clock. Pick the species you think will still be standing.

Sandbox

Scatter the world yourself, then watch the grass, the rot, and the hunters.

On the field

  • Sheep and rabbits eat the tall grass. Wolves hunt the sheep.
  • Falcons dive from above. Boars root for mushrooms and charge in a group.
  • Humans build shelters. Three of them will hunt a wolf. Different settlements fight.
  • Worms eat rot. Mushroom spores only take root where something has died.

While it runs

Right-drag orbits the field. Scroll zooms. WASD moves. Q and E rise and sink. The top-down button looks straight down.

Open the field

Spring grows the grass. Winter cuts it down and makes everyone hungrier. Rain, a predator call, and a meteor are spent from winnings, not from a free menu.
