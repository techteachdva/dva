Enter the Dreamscape

Play cooperatively as 1-6 Dreamers surviving and escaping a collapsing dreamscape. Chase the Archetype Quests, Gain enough Archetype points to wake up and win. Psyche cards are your power and health in the game, they represent your psychic abilities and your grasp on the dream reality. Lucidity is blue and it is your ability to see the landscapes and reveal the future, Elasticity is yellow and it is your movement through dreams, and Willpower is red and it is your ability to take actions and affect change. Each Dreamer is different, with passive bonuses to the three stats and an active special power. Work together as a team to escape before you never wake up from the dream.

Dream Settings

Same rules on every night. Daydream is the first one. Nap and Deep Sleep ask for more points.
