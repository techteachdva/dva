# Ainmos intro cinematic: script and captions

Draft for production. About 70 seconds. Same voice and cadence as the Somnia intro (`src/site/somnia/media/somnia-intro.vtt`): short lines, a beat of silence between ideas, each suit named as a single word.

Nothing here has been produced. The Somnia movie stays as it is; this is a second, Ainmos-only cinematic.

## Shot list

| # | Time | Picture | Line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0:00 | Black. A single hex of The Bed fades up, alone in the dark. | Every night, the dream rebuilds itself. |
| 2 | 0:04 | The camera pulls back: empty hex outlines shimmer around The Bed. | Tonight, you build it. |
| 3 | 0:07 | The Somnia wordmark flips in a mirror and reads AINMOS. | Welcome to Ainmos. |
| 4 | 0:10 | Three face-up Landscapes float in the Dreamscape Vault. | Lucidity. Choose what the dream becomes. |
| 5 | 0:14 | A tile glides from the Vault and snaps onto a glowing hex. Another, then another. | Lay it down, one Landscape at a time. Shape it. |
| 6 | 0:19 | Dreamers step across the new tiles. | Elasticity. Walk the dream you made. |
| 7 | 0:23 | A Dreambeast rises on a tile; three Psyche cards fan out. | Willpower. Meet what lives there. |
| 8 | 0:27 | The three cards light up as a Chord; extra dice tumble in. | Play your Psyche in Forms. Echo. Ladder. Chord. Each Form grows stronger. |
| 9 | 0:34 | The Night Bar across the top: face-down cards, a Boss face up at the end of each group. | Every third Dream, something waits at the end. |
| 10 | 0:38 | The Winged Turtle on The Bed, stacked three high. | A Boss. |
| 11 | 0:40 | The Boss falls; three Boon cards glow; one is taken. | Beat it, and keep what it guarded. |
| 12 | 0:44 | A dim shop of floating Objects: a ring, a candle, a conch. | Between dreams, the Lost and Found. Things you forgot you had. |
| 13 | 0:50 | Psyche cards drain from a hand. | Your Psyche is your life, and your coin. Spend it well. |
| 14 | 0:55 | The board dissolves back into the Vault; The Bed stands alone again. | When the dream runs out of places, it folds away and forms again. |
| 15 | 1:01 | The Night Bar climbs to Leviathan, rung 1. | Ten nightmares stand between you and waking. |
| 16 | 1:06 | The Bed glows. | Build the dream. Climb the night. Wake. |
