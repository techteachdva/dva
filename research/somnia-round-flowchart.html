Round structure & phase flow

The R.E.M. Round

One round, three phases, always in this order. There is no turn order inside a phase — the table acts together. One Dreamer opens each phase with 1 suited Psyche (or 1 Power Token), and everyone shares the budget.

The clock

The Dream Deck is the timer: 11 · 14 · 18 Dreams for Daydream, Nap, Deep Sleep. Bosses wake on Dreams 3, 6 and 9.

Drawing The Final Recurrence starts the ten-card endgame. Reach the goal before the deck runs dry.

Why you move

Quests name Landscapes. Stand on the tile to Draw its Mindstream or Meet the beast there next phase.

Beasts hurt the Dreamers next to them. Position is defense.

Leftover budget May

2 unused moves peek the next Dream. Leave it on top, or bury it ahead of Final Recurrence.

Fewer than 2, or pressing Next: Meet, forfeits the rest.

Wasteland

A Forgotten Landscape flips face-down. Its Encounter is Repressed and any Dreamer standing there loses 1 Psyche.

Wasteland can be Revealed again next round. The Bed is never Forgotten; if every other tile is, the Final Recurrence begins.

You may skip: press Next: Meet. Unused moves are forfeited. Two of them could have peeked the next Dream.

03 Meet Willpower
At start of phase Must

Each Dreamer on or adjacent to a roaming Dreambeast Represses 1 Psyche per such beast. Dreamers farther away pay nothing.

Bosses on The Bed tax whoever stands on The Bed or touches it. The tutorial skips this tax.

Open the phase May

One Dreamer spends 1 Willpower Psyche (or 1 Power Token). The same Dreamer cannot open two phases in a row unless they are alone or nobody else can pay.

Shared actions = card value + that Dreamer's Willpower

Dice battle. Your Power = Psyche played + matching stat + affinity + tokens. Roll that many d6 against the beast's Power d6. 5–6 succeed; most successes wins; ties go to the beast. After the dice settle, if you hold a Power Token, spend 1 once to subtract 1 beast success, or stand. Ties still favor the beast if you stand. Scripted tutorial battles skip this. Win: Accept (ally in hand) or Reject (reward). Lose: the play is spent, the beast stays.
