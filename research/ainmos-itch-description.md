# Ainmos — itch.io description (0.21 First Dreams)

**Ainmos** is a dreamcore roguelike card game. You are alone in a collapsing dream. Call the Archetypes, Acquire them, and wake up.

---

## One goal

Acquire Archetypes. **3** on a Daydream, **6** on a Nap, **all 12** in Deep Sleep. That is the whole night.

## A night of Cycles

Every Cycle is three Dreams, and every Dream is three phases: **Reveal · Explore · Meet**.

1. **A Dream.** It hurts. Endure it.
2. **A Dreambeast** lands on a Landscape of its suit. It chases you every Explore and bleeds you every dusk it stands. Beat it, or outrun it.
3. **An Archetype stirs.** Choose 1 of 3. It appears on one of its two home Landscapes and slips away from you every Explore.

When the last Cycle ends, the night doesn't. The **Long Night** keeps the dream going, each dusk a little colder, until you finish or forget yourself.

## Meeting an Archetype

Walk onto its Landscape and Meet it. First comes one of its **Events**:

- **Bright** (only if the Event's Landscape is placed): pay Psyche for a real edge in the fight, and Return lost cards from your Subconscious.
- **Dim**: free, and the fight gets harder.

Then play a spread of Psyche that makes a **Dream Form** (a pair, a run, three of a suit, one of each...) and roll against Power 12. Win, and the Archetype is yours: +1 toward waking, +2 Power Tokens, lost cards Returned, and its power for the rest of the night.

## Psyche is everything

Your Psyche cards are your health, your money, and your fighting hand. Spend Lucidity to build the dream, Elasticity to walk it, Willpower to act. Every card you lose sinks into the Subconscious; Archetypes, Bright Events, dawn, and banishing Dreambeasts bring them back.

## Glimmer and the Lost & Found

Nothing is wasted. Every Reveal, step, or action you leave unspent becomes **Glimmer ✧**. Between rounds the **Lost & Found** opens: Objects, Dream Form upgrades, and card Insights. Glimmer pays first.

## Build your night

- **Six Dreamers**, each with their own stats and power
- **45 Objects**, collectible **sets** that Acquire an Archetype on their own
- **Dream Forms** that level up, and **Psyche upgrades**: Vivid, Lucid, Recurring, Chaotic
- **Ten Dreambeasts** climbing from the Winged Turtle to Leviathan, each bending a rule
- **105 Events** woven into the 12 Archetypes and the 24 Landscapes they call home

## Plays anywhere

Desktop, tablet, and phone (landscape), mouse or touch. Saves on your device. Works offline once it has loaded.
