Escape the Dreamscape.

Six Dreamers have fallen asleep in the same bed and woken inside the same dream. Somewhere past the Endless Hallway and the Sea of Teeth, the Archetypes of the Unconscious are waiting to be claimed. Acquire enough of them and you wake with the dawn. Linger, and the Dreamscape collapses with you still inside it.

SOMNIA is a cooperative tabletop adventure for 1–6 players, played in your browser on desktop, iPad, or phone. One shared table, one shared fate.

How a dream is played

Reveal

Draw the Dream. Turn face-down Landscapes over one by one and watch the map bloom outward from The Bed — a new tile, a new Mindstream, a new place to get lost.

Explore

Spend Elasticity to move your Dreamer across the hexes. Every Landscape has a power and a price; the clever route is rarely the short one.

Meet

Face what lives in the Landscape. Dreambeasts test you, Encounters tempt you, Objects change the rules. Accept the offer or Reject it — both cost something.

The cards you carry

  • Psyche — your hand of Lucidity, Elasticity and Willpower. Spend them well; a Dreamer with an empty Psyche is one nightmare from waking up wrong.
  • Mindstreams — the three rivers of the mind, each with its own supply. Cycle cards through them to keep your hand alive.
  • Archetypes — the prize. Complete a Quest on the right Landscape and the Archetype is yours, along with its Power.
  • Dreambeasts, Allies & Objects — companions, curses and curiosities picked up along the way.
  • The Subconscious — everything you Repress sinks here. It does not stay quiet. Return cards before the pile grows into a nightmare of its own.

Will you control the dream?

Every round the Dream deck thins. Every Landscape revealed brings the Final Recurrence a step closer. Can you Acquire enough Archetypes to wake up before the Dreamscape collapses — or will you be cast adrift in an eternal night?

Play SOMNIA and find out.

What's on the table

Dreamers6 unique Dreamers, each with a signature Power
CardsHundreds of illustrated Landscapes, Archetypes, Dreambeasts, Objects and Psyche
SeedsEvery dream has a Game ID — share it and a friend gets the exact same shuffle. Six secret keyword seeds bend the rules.

Learn inTwo guided tutorial rounds with sparkles that show every tap

Fits in your pocket, fills the table

Somnia sizes itself to whatever it wakes up on. On a laptop the whole Dreamscape sprawls across the screen. On an iPad the hand rail and the Table drawer sit under your thumbs. On a phone the board shrinks to fit and the hand scrolls sideways. Rotate, and it re-fits itself. No settings to fiddle with — just sit down and dream.
